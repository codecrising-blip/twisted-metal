# Reference sounds (not game files)

Owner, session 6: these are **study material** to learn how the series sounds across the years, not resources to
drop into the game. Godot skips this folder (`.gdignore`), so nothing here is imported or shipped.

## voices/ - for writing and voicing lines later
- **Calypso's way of talking and his voice:** `calypso_talk_and_song.mp3` and the TM3 clips `tm3_axel`,
  `tm3_darkside`, `tm3_hollywood`, `tm3_minion`, `tm3_tokyo`, `tm3_washington_dc` (Calypso speaking), and
  `calypso_on_darkside`, `calypso_on_minion`, `calypso_on_london`, `calypso_on_egypt`, `calypso_on_blimp` (owner,
  round 12). His manner (owner): slow, arrogant, rage-baiting, sarcastic, elegant; rises and falls, stretches
  words ("WELCOMMMME to TWISted meTAL..."). His in-game lines are written with the owner later.
- **Warhawk fight intro** (reads your violations of the law, orders you to step out of the vehicle and surrender):
  `warhawk_intro_tmb_rooftop.mp3`.
- `tmb_minions_stadium.mp3` (Twisted Metal: Black). The owner: not all of these clips matter; better ones will follow.
